# Team Rankings

_Part of the design documents; the map is in the repository's [README](../../README.md)._

A separate ranking pool for teams from anywhere: tournament opponents, another
league, a club you are about to play. Only scores are entered — there are no box
scores here — and every team is rated against every other by the same
opponent-adjusted model the league uses.

What each part does, one document each:

- [Age groups](age-groups.md): which age a team is, and what is left out.
- [A page per age level](pages.md).
- [Scouting report](scouting.md).
- [How the two modes connect](two-modes.md): which club each League Standings team is.
- [Placeholders](placeholders.md): stand-ins, bracket slots, and the tidy that settles them.
- [What a pull left behind, and what to check](pool-health.md).
- [Names, the Games tab and importing](names-games-importing.md).
- [Pulling from GameChanger](pulling.md).
- [States, and the ratings a forecast reads](ratings.md): the fit, recent form, playing up and
  down, where a rating starts, and **The model, measured**.
